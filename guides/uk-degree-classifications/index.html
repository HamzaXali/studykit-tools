UK degree classifications explained

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

Undergraduate degrees in the United Kingdom and in many programmes modelled on UK practice are awarded in classes. Those labels are familiar to UK employers and postgraduate selectors, but they are not the same thing as a US GPA.
