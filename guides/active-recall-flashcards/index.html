Active recall with flashcards

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

Active recall means trying to retrieve an answer before you look it up. It is one of the most reliable study techniques for later exam performance, and flashcards are a convenient way to force that retrieval.

Why recall beats re-reading

Re-reading notes feels fluent but often creates an illusion of mastery; retrieval is harder and more useful.
