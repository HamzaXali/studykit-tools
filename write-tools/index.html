FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these writing tools free?

Yes. The word counter, reading time calculator, case converter, citation generator, whitespace cleaner and duplicate line remover are free and do not ask you to sign up.

Is my essay uploaded?

No. Counting, case changes and cleaning happen in your browser. The text is not sent to StudyKit Hub for processing.

Which citation styles can I generate?

The citation generator builds references for books, websites and journal articles in APA 7 and MLA 9. It does not cover every variation in those manuals, so check your department’s guide before you submit.

Will the word counter match my word processor exactly?

Not always. Hyphenated words and some punctuation are counted differently by different programs. If a submission portal shows its own count, use that figure for the final check.
