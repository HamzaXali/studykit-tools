How to calculate GPA (US-style)

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

Grade point average is common in the United States and in programmes that adopt a similar credit system. If you study in the UK or the Netherlands, you may still see GPA on transcripts for exchange or applications abroad — but local classifications differ.
