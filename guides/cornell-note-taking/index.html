Cornell note-taking method

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

The Cornell method splits a page into a cue column, a notes area and a summary. It turns raw lecture capture into something you can revise from, especially when paired later with flashcards or tagged digital notes.

Page layout

Draw a vertical cue column about a quarter of the page wide, a large notes area, and a summary band at the bottom.
