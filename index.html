Free text tools for students, teachers and writers

Six small tools for everyday study and writing jobs. They work on phones and computers, need no account, and process your text in your own browser.

Tools

  • Word and Character Counter

    Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and lines as you type. Works with emoji, accents, Urdu, Arabic and Chinese.

  • Reading Time Calculator

    Estimate how long a text takes to read silently or to say aloud, at a speed you choose.

  • Case Converter

    Switch text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case, with undo.

  • Duplicate Line Remover

    Delete repeated lines from a list while keeping the original order, and see how many were removed.

  • Whitespace Cleaner

    Fix messy spacing from PDFs and web pages: extra spaces, blank lines, tabs and invisible characters.

  • Study Notes Organiser

    Write, tag and search study notes that are saved only in your own browser. Export them any time.

What you can do here

  • Stay within a word limit. Use the word counter while you edit an essay or abstract.
  • Plan a talk or a reading task. The reading time calculator estimates how long a text takes to read or present.
  • Tidy copied text. The whitespace cleaner fixes spacing from PDFs, and the case converter fixes text typed in capitals.
  • Clean up lists. The duplicate line remover deletes repeats from class lists, vocabulary and references.
  • Keep revision notes together. The study notes organiser lets you tag and search notes saved in your browser.

How your text is handled

Every tool runs as a small program in your browser. The text you type or paste is not sent to this website or to anyone else. The notes organiser saves notes in your browser's own storage on your device, and nowhere else. Read the privacy policy for details.

Accessibility

The tools can be used with a keyboard and a screen reader, and they adapt to small screens and to your device's light or dark setting. If something does not work for you, please tell us.
