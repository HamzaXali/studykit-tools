FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does 1,000 words take to read?

With this site’s default speeds, 1,000 words take 4 min 13 sec to read silently at 238 words per minute, and 7 min 9 sec to speak at 140 words per minute. The calculator rounds each result up to the next second. You can enter any speed from 50 to 1,000 words per minute.

How do I count the days between two dates?

Enter the start date and the end date in the age and date calculator. It reports the gap in days and weeks, and it can also show an exact age in years, months and days. It does not know your timetable, so confirm the date on the exam notice or the assignment brief.

What is a Pomodoro session on this site?

The timer opens at 25 minutes of focus, then a 5 minute short break, and a 15 minute long break after every 4 focus sessions. You can change those lengths in the tool. The timer stays in the browser tab and does not upload anything.

Do I need an account to plan?

No. None of these tools ask you to sign up, and the text or dates you enter are handled in your browser.
