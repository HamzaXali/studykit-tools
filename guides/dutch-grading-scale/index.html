Dutch 1–10 grading scale explained

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

Universities in the Netherlands commonly mark on a scale from 1 to 10. Exchange students from the US or UK often need a careful read of what a “6” or “8” means locally, because the culture of the scale differs from letter grades and UK percentages.

How the scale is used

On the Dutch scale, 10 is outstanding and rare in many faculties; marks around 6 are a typical pass threshold, though programme rules can differ.
