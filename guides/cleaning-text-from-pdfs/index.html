Cleaning text copied from PDFs and slides

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

Copying from PDFs, slide decks and web articles often brings broken line breaks, double spaces and invisible characters. Cleaning that text before you quote or annotate it prevents messy notes and accidental plagiarism from hidden headers.

What goes wrong
