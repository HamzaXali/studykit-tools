These study tools are for the work that sits around the essay itself: keeping notes, turning them into questions, listening back, and protecting a block of time. The Pomodoro timer, the text to speech reader and the reading time calculator do not save a copy of your text on this website.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these study tools free?

Yes. Every study tool on this page is free. There is no trial, no paywall and no limit on how often you open them.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can open a tool and start straight away. There is no account, no email and no sign-up form.

Do my notes stay private?

Yes. Notes and flashcards are saved in your browser’s own storage on this device. StudyKit Hub does not receive the text you type. The privacy policy explains the optional newsletter form on the home page and cookieless analytics, which are separate from the tools.
