Spaced repetition for long-term memory

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

Spaced repetition schedules reviews at increasing intervals so you practise material again just as it becomes harder to remember. For semester-long courses, spacing usually beats cramming the night before.

The spacing idea

Memory strengthens when retrieval happens after some forgetting; immediate re-reading is weaker practice.
