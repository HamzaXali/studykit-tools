Pomodoro technique for exam revision

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

The Pomodoro approach — focused work blocks separated by short breaks — fits exam revision because it makes progress visible and reduces endless “one more page” sessions that drain attention.

Why short blocks help

Attention fades during long unstructured sessions; short blocks create natural stopping points for recall checks.
