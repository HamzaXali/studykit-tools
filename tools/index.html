Study tools

Six free tools for everyday study, writing and teaching tasks. They work on phones and computers, need no account, and process your text inside your own browser.

Revise and organise

  • Revise

    Study Notes Organiser

    Write, tag and search study notes that are saved only in your own browser. Export them as JSON, Markdown or plain text.

    Good for: revision notes by subject, reading summaries, lesson ideas.

Write and plan

  • Write

    Word and Character Counter

    Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and lines as you type, in English, Urdu, Arabic, Chinese and many other languages.

    Good for: essays and abstracts with a word limit, application forms with a character limit.

  • Plan

    Reading Time Calculator

    Estimate how long a text takes to read silently or to say aloud, at a speed you choose.

    Good for: timing a presentation, setting a reading task for a class.

Format and tidy text

  • Format

    Case Converter

    Switch text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case, with undo.

    Good for: essay titles, headings, references typed in capitals.

  • Tidy

    Whitespace Cleaner

    Remove extra spaces, blank lines, tabs and invisible characters from copied text.

    Good for: text copied from PDFs, slides and web pages.

  • Tidy

    Duplicate Line Remover

    Delete repeated lines from a list while keeping the original order, and see how many were removed.

    Good for: vocabulary lists, class lists, reference lists.

How the tools handle your text

Each tool is a small program that runs in your browser. Your text is not uploaded to this website or sent anywhere else. Only the study notes organiser saves anything, and it saves to your browser's own storage on your device. See the privacy policy for details.
