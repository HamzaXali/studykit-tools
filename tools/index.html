Study tools

Fourteen free tools for everyday study, writing and teaching tasks. They work on phones and computers, need no account, and process your text inside your own browser.

Revise and organise

  • Revise

    Study Notes Organiser

    Write, tag and search study notes that are saved only in your own browser. Export them as JSON, Markdown or plain text.

    Good for: revision notes by subject, reading summaries, lesson ideas.

  • Revise

    Flashcards Maker

    Make flashcard decks and practise them with flip, know and review. Saved only in your own browser.

    Good for: vocabulary, definitions, formulas, dates.

  • Focus

    Pomodoro Study Timer

    Study in focused blocks with short and long breaks. Start, pause, reset, with an optional sound or notification.

    Good for: revision sessions, homework, long reading tasks.

  • Listen

    Text to Speech Reader

    Listen to notes or an essay draft read aloud by your browser’s built-in voices, at the speed you choose.

    Good for: proofreading essays, revising on the move, language listening practice.

Grades, marks and dates

  • Grades

    GPA and CGPA Calculator

    Work out semester GPA from credit hours and grade points, update your CGPA, and convert CGPA to a percentage.

    Good for: semester results, scholarship and admission forms that ask for a percentage.

  • Grades

    Percentage and Marks Calculator

    Turn marks into a percentage, total several subjects, and work out the marks you need for a target.

    Good for: exam results, report cards, admission and scholarship forms.

  • Grades

    Final Grade Calculator

    Find the score you need on your final from your current grade, the final’s weight and your target.

    Good for: finals week planning, “what if I get an A/B/C”, syllabus-weight courses.

  • Plan

    Age and Date Difference Calculator

    Find an exact age in years, months and days, or count the days and weeks between two dates.

    Good for: admission age limits, exam countdowns, project deadlines.

Write and plan

  • Write

    Word and Character Counter

    Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and lines as you type, in English, Urdu, Arabic, Chinese and many other languages.

    Good for: essays and abstracts with a word limit, application forms with a character limit.

  • Plan

    Reading Time Calculator

    Estimate how long a text takes to read silently or to say aloud, at a speed you choose.

    Good for: timing a presentation, setting a reading task for a class.

  • Write

    Citation Generator (APA 7 and MLA 9)

    Make APA 7 and MLA 9 references for books, websites and journal articles, then copy them.

    Good for: essays, assignments, theses and lesson materials.

Format and tidy text

  • Format

    Case Converter

    Switch text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case, with undo.

    Good for: essay titles, headings, references typed in capitals.

  • Tidy

    Whitespace Cleaner

    Remove extra spaces, blank lines, tabs and invisible characters from copied text.

    Good for: text copied from PDFs, slides and web pages.

  • Tidy

    Duplicate Line Remover

    Delete repeated lines from a list while keeping the original order, and see how many were removed.

    Good for: vocabulary lists, class lists, reference lists.

How the tools handle your text

Each tool is a small program that runs in your browser. Your text is not uploaded to this website or sent anywhere else. Only the study notes organiser and the flashcards maker save anything, and they save to your browser's own storage on your device. The text to speech reader uses your browser's own voices; some browsers offer online voices, which are labelled in the voice list. See the privacy policy for details.
