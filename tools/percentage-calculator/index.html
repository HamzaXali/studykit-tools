How to use the percentage calculator

  1. Enter the marks you obtained and the total marks. The percentage appears straight away.
  2. To find an overall result, add each subject's marks and total in Total of several subjects.
  3. To plan ahead, enter a target percentage and the total marks to see how many marks you need.
  4. To see progress, compare an earlier and a later percentage.

The formulas

Percentage = marks obtained ÷ total marks × 100.

Marks needed = target percentage ÷ 100 × total marks. Round up, because a mark short of the target is not enough.

Difference is the later result minus the earlier one, in percentage points. Relative change = difference ÷ earlier result × 100.

Worked examples

Board exam result

You scored 942 out of 1,100. Percentage = 942 ÷ 1,100 × 100 = 85.64%.

Progress between two tests

Your score went from 60% to 75%. That is 15 percentage points higher, and a 25% relative improvement.

Questions and answers

Why are percentage points different from percent change?
Percentage points are a simple subtraction (75 − 60 = 15 points). Percent change compares the difference with where you started (15 ÷ 60 = 25%).
Can I enter half marks?
Yes. Decimals such as 47.5 work in every box.
Is anything saved or uploaded?
No. The calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
