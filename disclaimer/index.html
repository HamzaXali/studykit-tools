Counts and estimates

Word, character and sentence counts follow the rules described on each tool page. Other software, and the rules of your school, university, publisher or exam board, may count differently. Reading and speaking times are estimates based on average speeds. When a limit matters, check how the person setting it will count.

Converted and cleaned text

Case conversion cannot recognise every name, abbreviation or special term, and text cleaning can change layout. Review converted text before you submit or publish it.

Saved notes

Notes are stored in your browser and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Keep exported backups of anything important.
