Readability scores explained

Study guide · Updated 28 October 2026

Readability formulas turn sentence length and word complexity into a score. They can flag prose that is denser than you intended, but they do not judge argument quality — and they are built for English.

What the scores measure

Most common school formulas count syllables, words and sentences to estimate how hard a passage is to read.
