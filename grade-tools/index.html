Grade Tools

Calculate a GPA, CGPA, percentage or the score you need on a final.

🔍

Pakistani universities that use a semester system usually report a GPA for the term and a CGPA across the degree. Many follow Higher Education Commission (HEC) guidance, but the grade-point scale and any conversion to a percentage are set by the institution, not by one national formula. Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE) and school results are often marks out of a total, which convert to a percentage by dividing obtained marks by the total.

Use the GPA and CGPA calculator with the points on your transcript, and the percentage and marks calculator for marks-based results. The final grade calculator answers a different question: the score required on a weighted final. The UK degree classification calculator is for First, 2:1, 2:2 and Third, which is not the same system. Grading scales vary. Check your own institution before you rely on a number for an application. A longer walk-through is in what do I need on the final and how to calculate GPA.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is GPA calculated?

On a credit-hour scale, multiply each course’s grade points by its credit hours, add those quality points, then divide by the total credit hours. Example: 3 credits at 4.0, 3 credits at 3.33 and 2 credits at 2.67 give 27.33 quality points over 8 credits, which is a semester GPA of 3.42 on a 4.0 scale. The calculator also offers a 5.0 scale and a 10-point scale. Use the grade points on your transcript. Universities, including those that follow HEC semester guidance in Pakistan, do not all use the same scale.

What is CGPA?

CGPA means cumulative grade point average: previous quality points plus this semester’s quality points, divided by all credit hours so far. If you already have a CGPA of 3.0 over 32 credits and this semester is 3.5 over 16 credits, the combined CGPA is 3.17 when rounded to two decimal places. A first semester has no earlier CGPA to combine.

How to convert marks to percentage in Pakistan?

For a marks-based result, including many BISE board and school results, divide the marks obtained by the total marks and multiply by 100. 850 out of 1,100 is (850 ÷ 1,100) × 100, which is 77.27% when rounded to two decimal places. Converting a CGPA to a percentage is a different step, and universities publish different formulas. The GPA calculator can divide CGPA by the scale and multiply by 100, or apply a multiplier you type in. Check your institution before you put a percentage on a form.
