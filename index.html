Free study tools

Every tool works on phones and computers and processes your text inside your own browser.

  • Revise

    Study Notes Organiser

    Write, tag and search study notes that are saved only in your own browser. Export them any time.

  • Write

    Word and Character Counter

    Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and lines as you type. Works with emoji, accents, Urdu, Arabic and Chinese.

  • Plan

    Reading Time Calculator

    Estimate how long a text takes to read silently or to say aloud, at a speed you choose.

  • Format

    Case Converter

    Switch text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case, with undo.

  • Tidy

    Whitespace Cleaner

    Fix messy spacing from PDFs and web pages: extra spaces, blank lines, tabs and invisible characters.

  • Tidy

    Duplicate Line Remover

    Delete repeated lines from a list while keeping the original order, and see how many were removed.

  • Grades

    GPA and CGPA Calculator

    Work out semester GPA from credit hours and grade points, update your CGPA, and convert CGPA to a percentage.

  • Grades

    Percentage and Marks Calculator

    Turn marks into a percentage, total several subjects, and work out the marks you need for a target.

  • Revise

    Flashcards Maker

    Make flashcard decks and practise them with flip, know and review. Saved only in your own browser.

  • Focus

    Pomodoro Study Timer

    Study in focused blocks with short and long breaks. Start, pause, reset, with an optional sound or notification.

  • Write

    Citation Generator (APA 7 and MLA 9)

    Make APA 7 and MLA 9 references for books, websites and journal articles, then copy them.

  • Plan

    Age and Date Difference Calculator

    Find an exact age in years, months and days, or count the days and weeks between two dates.

  • Listen

    Text to Speech Reader

    Listen to notes or an essay draft read aloud by your browser’s built-in voices, at the speed you choose.

What you can do here

  • Keep revision notes together. The study notes organiser lets you tag and search notes saved in your browser, and export a backup.
  • Stay within a word limit. Use the word counter while you edit an essay, report or abstract.
  • Plan a talk or a reading task. The reading time calculator estimates how long a text takes to read or present.
  • Tidy copied text. The whitespace cleaner fixes spacing from PDFs and slides, and the case converter fixes headings typed in capitals.
  • Check your grades. Work out your GPA and CGPA with the GPA calculator, or turn exam marks into a percentage with the percentage calculator.
  • Revise actively. Test yourself with flashcards, study in focused blocks with the Pomodoro timer, and listen to your notes with the text to speech reader.
  • Reference your sources. The citation generator formats books, web pages and journal articles in APA 7 or MLA 9.
  • Count the days. The age and date calculator finds an exact age or the days until an exam.
  • Clean up lists. The duplicate line remover deletes repeats from vocabulary lists, class lists and reference lists.

How your text is handled

Every tool runs as a small program in your browser. The text you type or paste is not sent to this website or to anyone else. The notes organiser and the flashcards maker save in your browser's own storage on your device, and nowhere else. Read the privacy policy for details.

Accessibility

The tools are built to work with a keyboard and assistive technology, and they adapt to small screens and to your device's light or dark setting. They are checked with automated accessibility tests and keyboard testing; they have not yet been tested with every screen reader. If something does not work for you, please tell us.
